# Concepts

Read the first three pages together: they establish what a configuration is,
where its inputs come from, and how nested values are combined. Later pages
describe independent capabilities that build on those rules.

1. [Configuration models](configuration-models.md): validation, options, freezing,
   copying, and explicit mutation.
1. [Sources and inheritance](sources-and-inheritance.md): precedence and provenance.
1. [Nested configuration](nested-configuration.md): mapping merges and model baselines.
1. [Input annotations](input-annotations.md): adapting strings and evaluating directives.
1. [CLIs](clis.md): settings parsers, nested fields, and commands.
1. [Factories and templates](factories-and-templates.md): generation and resolution.
1. [Contexts](contexts.md): activating configuration within an execution context.
1. [Configured objects](configured-objects.md): durable inputs and runtime resources.
1. [Serialization](serialization.md): documents, concrete types, and format helpers.
